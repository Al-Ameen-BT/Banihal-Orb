Active Directory authentication (optional)

When enabled, users not found locally are authenticated against AD and mapped to a role by group membership. Local accounts always work — AD is never required, and if every domain controller is unreachable you can still sign in locally. MFA still applies on top of AD.

If LDAPS on 636 is reset by the domain controller, it usually has no LDAPS certificate installed. StartTLS on 389 still encrypts credentials and often works where LDAPS does not.

Group → role mapping

Enter the group name or full DN. Most privileged match wins. A user in none of these is denied.
